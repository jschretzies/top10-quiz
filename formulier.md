# Pre-meeting form (for the finale)

Send this to your colleagues a few days before the meeting, for example in Microsoft Forms or just in a Teams chat.
Ask for **exact numbers**: that makes ties less likely and the ranking more fun.

---

**Subject: 12 quick questions for Friday's quiz 🎉**

Hi all! For the finale of our team quiz I need a few numbers from you. Takes 3 minutes, no right or wrong answers.
Please give an exact number (so "37", not "about 40"). Send them to me before [date].

1. How many minutes is your commute to the office (one way)?
2. How many unread emails are in your inbox right now?
3. How many browser tabs do you have open right now?
4. How many meetings are in your calendar this week?
5. How many cups of coffee and tea do you drink in a normal work week?
6. How many countries have you visited?
7. How many months have you worked at Dexcom?
8. How many photos are on your phone right now?
9. How many calls or Teams meetings did you have yesterday?
10. How many minutes does your lunch break usually take?
11. How many days did you work from home last month?
12. How many steps is it from your desk to the coffee machine? (Count them next time, or make a guess.)

Thanks! 🙌

---

## For you (the host)

- Before the meeting: open the game, press → and type everyone's first name on the "Who's playing?" screen. Then press **I** and enter all answers. The game makes the ranking itself (highest number = place 1).
- In the same screen, tick 1 to 3 finale questions. Each one becomes a list in the finale. Pick the ones with the biggest differences between people.
- Did two people give the same number? The game shows a tie warning. Tied colleagues share the place, and either order counts as correct.
- The question wording here matches `data.js`. If you change a question, change it in both places.
